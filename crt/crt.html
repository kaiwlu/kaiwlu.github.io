    How to play+

    Find the hidden integer t in at most 6 guesses.

    For each guess x, use the current prime p. The game returns the remainder

    (t − x) mod p.

    Use the previous remainders to narrow down the possibilities. A remainder of zero means your guess and the target agree modulo that prime; keep going until you find the exact number.
